Captions that move at the reciter’s pace.

This is a working model of the caption engine. Every style below runs on the same timing rules the iOS app will use. Chunks appear 0.2 s before they are recited, hand over without blank frames, and fade out during long pauses. The footage is simulated and there is no audio; word timings are modelled on a measured recitation of Al-Fatiha.

Timing and motion

The upper lane plots how visible each caption is over time, with the current style’s easing. The lower lane shows the recited words. Ayahs 4→5 are separated by a long pause, so that caption fades out instead of handing over.

Caption on screen (Arabic opacity)Word recited

show = first word − 0.20 s. A caption arrives just ahead of its first word, so viewers read along instead of chasing the audio.

handover = next show − 0.18 s. The outgoing caption starts fading 0.18 s before the next one enters. The two are never both at full strength, and there is no empty frame between them.

Silence longer than 1.5 s means the caption holds for 0.8 s after the last word, then fades out. Every caption is fully visible for at least 1.2 s.

Brand

Rattil comes from Al-Muzzammil 73:4, “and recite the Qur’an with measured recitation.” The mark is a mihrab arch with lines of text rising inside it. Lamp Gold is the only accent colour.
